# Code of Conduct

This is a short policy. Read it once and move on.

## What we expect

- Be direct without being cruel.
- Disagree on technical merits, not on people.
- Assume good faith.
- Accept criticism gracefully. Give it specifically.
- Help newcomers when you can. Most of us were beginners six months ago.

## What we don't tolerate

- Personal attacks, slurs, or harassment.
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Demeaning language directed at any group based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, nationality, or appearance.
- Persistent disruption of discussions.
- Trolling or bad-faith arguments.

## How to report

Email hello@augmex.io with the subject line `[CoC] Senior Board Audit`. Include:

- Where the incident happened (issue, PR, email thread).
- What was said or done.
- Whether you would like the reporter to be anonymous.

Reports are reviewed by Augmex Technologies maintainers. We respond within 7 days.

## Consequences

Depending on severity:

1. Private clarification or warning.
2. Public warning on the relevant thread.
3. Temporary suspension from the project.
4. Permanent ban.

We use judgment. We will not apply rule 4 for a single bad day, but we will apply it for repeated bad behaviour or for a single egregious incident.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication tied to the project, including emails sent to maintainers.

It does not apply to personal conduct outside the project, unless that conduct directly affects the safety of project participants.
